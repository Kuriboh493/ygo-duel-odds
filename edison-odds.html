    Card details

    "Set chance" is how readily a player sets this card face-down instead of playing it. For less common cards these values are guessed from card text, so adjust any that look off. Cards marked "not recognized" need a type picked.

    How the odds are worked out

    Every card in their list that you have not seen is equally likely to be anywhere hidden: in their hand, face-down on the field, or in their deck. That alone gives the standard draw odds.

    Face-down cards add rules. A set monster has to be a monster, and a set spell/trap has to be a spell or trap. Players also choose what they set, so traps are weighted as likely sets while cards like Allure of Darkness are not. Each "no response" you log lowers the weight of cards that would have answered.

    Any decklist works. Staples use hand-tuned values. Every other card's type comes from the YGOPRODeck card database, and its set chance and response tags are guessed from its text. If the opponent reveals a card that isn't in their list, it's added to the list automatically.

    The page then simulates 30,000 deals of their unseen cards that fit everything you logged, weights each deal by how well it fits, and reports the share where each card lands in each spot. Only information visible to both players goes in.

    Known limits: it does not model what a player would choose to discard or keep. When a card leaves their hand face-down, it treats it as the most recently drawn card for hand evidence purposes.
