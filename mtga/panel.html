    What the model would do

    It ranks the plays Arena offers by how likely a player in its training games was to make them. It only suggests, and never touches Arena. It works against Arena's built-in bot and in replays, and stays locked in games against people.

    "No response" evidence

    When your spell resolves or your attack connects while they have mana up and make no play, cards they could have paid for become less likely. Passing is sometimes a choice, so this is a judgment call.

    About this log

    How the odds are worked out

    The tool reads Arena's Player.log file on this computer. It uses what both players can see: their hand and library sizes, cards that have been public, and cards revealed to you. It doesn't use anything else in the log about their hidden cards.

    With no decklist pasted, their deck is a mix of archetypes from recent tournament decklists, plus decks you've faced, with earlier games in the same match counting most. Every card they show shifts the mix toward decks that run it.

    Each simulated deal draws a whole decklist from that mix, then deals their hand from it. The odds are the share of deals where each card lands in each spot.

    A threat only counts if the mana they have open can pay its normal cost, and a board wipe counts if they could pay for it next turn with one more land. Alternate costs, convoke and cost reducers aren't modeled, and neither is the land mix players choose to keep in an opening hand.
