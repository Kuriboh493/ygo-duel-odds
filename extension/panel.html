    How the odds are worked out

    The extension reads the public state of the duel from DuelingBook as it happens: how many cards are in their hand and deck, every face-down card, and every card that has been face-up or revealed. It never reads the names of cards in their hand or face-down unless DuelingBook showed them to both players.

    The archetypes come from two sources: Goat and Edison decks that topped events, from Format Library, and decks published on YGOPRODeck. Topping decks count most. Each deck type from Format Library becomes an archetype. Published decks fill in copy counts and casual archetypes. At the start of a duel, each archetype's weight is mostly its share of topping lists. Without this data, the panel falls back to rough archetype sketches.

    With no decklist pasted, their deck is guessed. The guess starts as a mix of those archetypes, plus decks you've faced before, with earlier games against the same opponent counting most. Each archetype gives every card a chance of running 0 to 3 copies. Every card they show shifts the mix toward decks that run it. Every card that hasn't appeared yet, as more of their deck gets seen, makes it a little less likely they run many copies. Cards no known deck runs are allowed for too.

    Every unseen card is equally likely to be anywhere hidden: their hand, face-down on the field, face-down banished, or the deck. Set monsters must be monsters and set spells/traps must be spells or traps, weighted by how often each card gets set. Each "no response" lowers the weight of cards that would have answered. A face-down card that goes back to their hand unseen keeps its "was set" weighting.

    The panel simulates thousands of deals. Each one first draws a whole decklist from the guess, then deals out their hidden cards, and is weighted by how well it fits everything so far. The odds are the share of deals where each card lands in each spot.
