    How the odds are worked out

    The extension reads the public state of the duel from DuelingBook as it happens: how many cards are in their hand and deck, every face-down card, and every card that has been face-up or revealed. It never reads the names of cards in their hand or face-down unless DuelingBook showed them to both players.

    Every card in their list that you haven't seen is equally likely to be anywhere hidden: their hand, face-down on the field, face-down banished, or the deck. Set monsters must be monsters and set spells/traps must be spells or traps, weighted by how often each card gets set. Each "no response" lowers the weight of cards that would have answered.

    A face-down card that goes back to their hand unseen keeps its "was set" weighting. A card that is revealed and then returned to their hand is counted as known.

    The panel then simulates 30,000 deals of their unseen cards that fit everything so far, weights each deal by how well it fits, and reports the share where each card lands in each spot.
